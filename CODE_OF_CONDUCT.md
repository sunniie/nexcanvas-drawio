# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in NexCanvas respectful, inclusive, and
free from harassment for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background,
appearance, disability, nationality, race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual
orientation, or other personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and respectful.
- Critique ideas and artifacts, not people.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Respect privacy, licenses, trademarks, and confidential information.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope while remaining free to fork the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized content, deliberate
misrepresentation, publication of private information, and sustained disruptive
conduct are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report sensitive conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact options
on the [project owner's GitHub profile](https://github.com/sunniie). Maintainers may
edit or remove content, issue warnings, temporarily limit participation, or ban
participants when necessary to protect the community.

This policy applies to repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and public
events where a person represents the project.
